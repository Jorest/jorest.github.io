AR-Invaders

AR-Invaders is a short AR roguelike where you scan a wall, open a portal, and fight off alien ships in your own space. Every wave gets harder, and after each one, you pick an upgrade to help you survive just a bit longer. Built with Unity and AR Foundation.

Elements Together (XR Hack 2024)

Mixed reality co-op game designed to be experienced with friends in the same physical space. Each player has unique elemental powers they can cast by using different hand gesture. To defeat your enemies, you need to collaborate with your friend, combining the elemental attacks.
