About Me

Hello! My name is Jorge. I’m a Game Programmer and Designer from Guatemala, currently based in Trondheim. I’m passionate about building interactive experiences, from concept to polished implementation, and I enjoy both creating new ideas and analyzing how games work under the hood.

My main focus is on gameplay programming, narrative-driven design, and player experience, with additional experience in VR/AR and experimental interaction design. I’m curious about emerging areas like mixed reality, gesture recognition, and data-driven simulation. I thrive in collaborative, interdisciplinary teams and enjoy bringing mechanics, systems, and stories together to create meaningful player choices.

When I’m not programming, you’ll probably find me exploring new music, tinkering with PC hardware, or hanging out with dogs.
