About Me

Hello! My name is Jorge. I am a XR Developer and Researcher. I am passionate about building robust interactive systems, from initial concept design to highly optimized implementation. I enjoy both pioneering new technical ideas and conducting rigorous analysis of how game systems influence player experience.

My core focus is on system architecture, core programming, and designing user-centric interactive experiences. I have specialized experience in Extended Reality (XR) systems (VR/AR) and experimental interaction design for simulation and education. I am particularly curious about emerging areas like: Adaptive Systems in Mixed Reality (MR), gesture recognition, and optimization-based simulation. I thrive in collaborative, interdisciplinary teams and enjoy connecting mechanics and data to create meaningful player choices.

When I am not prototyping systems, you will probably find me exploring new music, tinkering with PC hardware, or hanging out with dogs.
